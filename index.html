Our story

Why Wild Lirt Studio exists.

We wanted bots that didn't ask you to install anything new — just link them to WhatsApp, the app you already use every day, and get real utility back in return.

Built by Nullforge and friends, Wild Lirt Studio is a small, growing catalog of bots — each built for a different job, all sharing the same simple pairing flow.

Legal

Terms & Privacy

Using our bots

By pairing a bot, you're linking it as a companion device to your WhatsApp account. You can unpair at any time from your Dashboard or directly in WhatsApp's Linked Devices settings.

What we store

Session credentials needed to keep your bot connected, and basic usage stats to keep things running reliably. We don't sell your data.

Your responsibility

Don't use any bot for spam, harassment, or anything that violates WhatsApp's own terms of service — accounts doing so risk being restricted by WhatsApp itself, independent of us.
